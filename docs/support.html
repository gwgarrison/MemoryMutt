Getting Started

Open the Decks tab and add a starter deck — World Capitals, US State Capitals, US Presidents, Spanish, or French — or tap + to create your own. Then head to Study. MemoryMutt schedules each card with spaced repetition, so cards you know well come back less often and cards you miss come back sooner.

Study Modes

  • Flashcard — see the question, recall the answer, then mark it right or wrong.
  • Multiple Choice — pick the answer from four options.
  • True / False — decide whether the answer shown is correct.
  • Match Game — pair questions with their answers.
  • Hangman — spell out the answer letter by letter.
  • Speed Round — answer as many as you can against the clock.
  • Rescue Round — focused practice for "trouble cards" you keep missing.

Importing Cards from a CSV File

In Settings → Import from CSV, choose a file with a front column, a back column, and an optional hint column. Commas, semicolons, tabs, and pipes are all detected automatically. For example:

front,back,hint
Bonjour,Hello,
la biblioteca,the library,Where books live

You can export any deck back to CSV from Settings → Export Data.

Apple Watch

The Watch app studies the cards that are due on your iPhone. Keep your iPhone nearby when you open MemoryMutt on your watch; if it says it can't reach your iPhone, open MemoryMutt on the iPhone and tap Refresh. Answers you give on the watch are sent back to your iPhone, even if it was out of range at the time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my decks sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Not currently. MemoryMutt keeps all of your data on the device where you created it. Use CSV export and import to move a deck between devices.

Is there an account, subscription, or ads?
No. MemoryMutt has no accounts, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a deck from the Decks tab, or delete the app to remove everything.
