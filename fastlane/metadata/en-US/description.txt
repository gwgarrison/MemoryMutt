MemoryMutt helps you learn anything — and remember it. Build your own flashcard decks or start with a ready-made one, and MemoryMutt's spaced repetition schedules every card for the moment you're about to forget it, so your study time goes where it counts.

SEVEN WAYS TO STUDY
• Flashcard — recall the answer, then check yourself
• Multiple Choice — pick the right answer from four
• True / False — is the answer shown correct?
• Match Game — pair questions with their answers
• Hangman — spell the answer one letter at a time
• Speed Round — beat the clock
• Rescue Round — targeted practice for the cards you keep missing

SPACED REPETITION THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
MemoryMutt uses the proven SM-2 algorithm. Cards you know well come back less often; cards you miss come back sooner. Each card moves from New to Learning to Review to Mastered as you go.

TROUBLE CARDS, RESCUED
MemoryMutt notices the cards that keep slipping away and flags them as Trouble Cards. A Rescue Round works through them until they stick — and lets you add a hint when one keeps tripping you up.
